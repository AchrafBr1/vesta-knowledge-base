<summary>Specifications</summary>

#### Technical features

* 1/2.7" 8 MP CMOS sensor with progressive scan
* Maximum resolution 8MP (3840x2160)
* Colour and B/W day/night mode
* Minimum illumination of 0.007 lux in colour, 0.0007 lux in B/W and 0 lux with illuminator on
* Smart Dual Light with 40 m IR and 40 m white light
* Red and blue alarm light
* Motorised lens varifocal from 2.7 mm to 13.5 mm
* Aperture F1.6
* Angle of view 109° to 30° horizontal, 56° to 17° vertical and 131° to 35° diagonal

#### Video Stream

* H.264H, H.264M, H.264B and H.265 compression
* H.264H+, H.264M+, H.264B+ and H.265+ intelligent compression
* 3 streams
* Stream 1: 8MP@20 ips
* Stream 2: D1@25/30 ips
* Stream 3: 720P@25/30 ips
* CBR and VBR bitrate control
* Video bitrate from 3 Kbps to 20480 Kbps in H.264 and H.265

#### Functionalities

* BLC, HLC, 120 dB WDR, digital WDR and 3D-NR
* White balance
* Automatic and manual gain control
* Configurable motion detection in 4 rectangular zones
* 180° image rotation
* Mirror
* Privacy mask in 4 zones
* Illuminator control in automatic or manual mode
* Arming and disarming

#### Intelligent functions

* Motion detection with person and vehicle classification
* Intrusion detection with person, vehicle and non-motorised classification
* Line crossing detection with classification of persons, vehicles and non-motorised vehicles
* AI search via VSS

#### Active deterrence

* Audible alarm
* Luminous alarm with red and blue LED

#### Audio

* Built-in microphone
* Built-in speaker
* Two-way audio
* G.711a, G.711Mu, G.726 and G.726ASF audio compression

#### Storage

* FTP
* MicroSD card slot up to 256 GB in FAT32 (card not included)

#### Alarms

* Alarm events for disconnection, IP conflict, no SD card, capacity warning and SD card error

#### Network

* RJ45 10/100 Base-T network interface
* Protocols TCP/IP, UDP, HTTP, IGMP, ICMP, DHCP, RTP, RTSP, DNS, DDNS, FTP, NTP, PPPOE, UPNP and SMTP
* ONVIF, CGI and P2P support
* SDK and API
* Up to 20 users with 80M total bandwidth
* Compatible with IE8 and later, Chrome and Firefox
* PC software and app
* Mobile app for iOS and Android

#### Power supply

* Power supply 12 V DC (±30 %) or PoE 802.3af
* Basic power consumption 2.5 W
* Maximum power consumption 6.2 W

#### Environmental

* Operating temperature -30 °C to 60 °C (-22 °F to 140 °F)
* Storage temperature -40 °C to 60 °C (-40 °F to 140 °C)
* Relative humidity less than 95 %.
* IP67 protection

#### Physical characteristics

* Metal housing
* Dimensions: 110 x Ø122 mm
* Net weight: 670 g
