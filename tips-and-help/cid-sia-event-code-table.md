## Event Descriptions

### 100 – Medical

* When a device set to Medical attribute is triggered.

### 101 – Personal Emergency

* When a device set to Personal Emergency attribute is triggered.

### 102 – Personal Emergency

* When a device set to Personal Emergency attribute is triggered.

### 110 – Fire Emergency

* When a device set to Fire attribute is triggered.

### 111 – Smoke Emergency

* When Smoke Detector (SD) is triggered.
* When Smoke alarm is restored.

### 114 – Heat Emergency

* When Heat Detector (HD) is triggered.
* When Heat alarm is restored.

### 118 – Near Alarm

* When the Smoke Detector (SD) set to Fire Verification is first triggered.

### 120 – Panic

* When a device set to Panic attribute is pressed.

### 121 – Duress

* When the Duress Code is entered to disarm or arm the system.

### 122 – Silent Panic

* When a device set to Silent Panic is pressed.

### 130 – Burglar

* When an Instant Burglar Alarm is triggered.
* When an Instant Burglar Alarm is restored.

### 131 – Burglar Perimeter

* When the timer in Away Arm Mode expires without disarming the system.
* When a Delay Burglar Alarm Device is triggered during Away Entry Time and the timer expires without disarming the system.

### 132 – Burglar Interior

* When Entry Time is triggered in Home Arm Mode and the timer expires without disarming the system.
* When a Delay Burglar Alarm Device is triggered during Home Entry Time and the timer expires without disarming the system.

### 133 – Burglar (24H)

* Whenever a device set as 24H Burglar Alarm is triggered.

### 136 – Burglar Outdoor

* Whenever a device set as Burglar Outdoor is triggered.

### 137 – Panel Tamper

* When the Panel’s tamper switch is triggered.
* When the Panel’s tamper switch is restored.

### 139 – Burglar Verified

* When a sensor set to Cross Zone attribute verifies an alarm.

### 144 – IR Motion Sensor Lens Obstructed

* When masking event is detected for motion sensor’s lens(es).

### 147 – Supervision Failure (Sensor)

* When the Panel fails to receive supervision signals from a device within the preset supervision timer.
* When the device’s supervision failure status is cleared.

### 151 – Gas Emergency

* When a gas alarm is triggered.
* When the gas alarm is restored.

### 154 – Water Emergency

* When a water alarm is triggered.
* When the water alarm is restored.

### 158 – High Temperature Alarm

* When high temperature alarm is triggered.
* When high temperature alarm is restored.

### 159 – Low Temperature Alarm

* When low temperature alarm is triggered.
* When low temperature alarm is restored.

### 162 – Carbon Monoxide Emergency

* When a Carbon Monoxide alarm is triggered.
* When the Carbon Monoxide alarm is restored.

### 170 – High Power Consumption

* When high power consumption alarm is triggered.

### 171 – High Humidity Alarm

* When high humidity alarm is triggered.

### 172 – Low Humidity Alarm

* When low humidity alarm is triggered.

### 175/176 – Low Humidity Alarm

* When low humidity alarm is triggered.
* When low humidity alarm is restored.

### 301 – Panel AC Failure

* When the time of Control Panel’s AC power failure exceeds the set AC Fail Report period.
* When AC power is restored to the Control Panel.

### 302 – Panel Low Battery

* When the Panel’s battery voltage is low.
* When the Panel’s battery voltage is back to normal.

### 311 – Panel Battery Disconnected / Reconnected

* When the Panel’s battery is not connected.
* When the Panel’s battery is re-connected.

### 312 – Short Circuit Detected / Power Output Restored

* When the Panel detects short circuit and stops supplying power to voltage output terminals.
* When the Panel restores power for voltage output terminals.

### 337 – External Power Status

* When the Panel loses external power connection.
* When the Panel restores external power connection.

### 344 – Interference / Interference Problem Solved

*   **Cudy router operates through 4G**

    the Cudy router communicates exclusively via 4G

### 351 – GSM No Signal / GSM OK

* When the Panel’s cellular network is lost.
* When the Panel’s cellular network is restored.

### 353 – WIFI Disconnected / Reconnected

* When the Panel’s WIFI connection is lost.
* When the Panel’s WIFI connection is restored.

### 357 – Ethernet Network Failure

* When the IP network is lost.
* When the IP network is restored.

### 358 – Ethernet Cable Unplugged

* When the Ethernet cable is disconnected.
* When the Ethernet cable is reconnected.

### 364 – Normal Level of Carbon Dioxide

* When normal level of Carbon Dioxide (CO2) is detected.

### 365 – Low Level of Carbon Dioxide

* When low level of Carbon Dioxide (CO2) is detected.

### 366 – High Level of Carbon Dioxide

* When high level of Carbon Dioxide (CO2) is detected.

### 370 – TAG Operation

* When the keypad tag is used to Arm/Home Arm/Disarm the system.

### 374 – Force Arm

* When the system is force armed with fault events existed.

### 378 – Cross Zone Trouble

* When the Cross Zone Timer expires without any other sensor triggered.

### 380 – Device AC Failure / Wired Zone Abnormal State

* When an AC-powered device loses AC power connection.
* When a wired sensor sends fault or anti-masking event.

### 383 – Device Tamper

* When a device’s tamper switch is triggered.
* When the device’s tamper switch is restored.

### 384 – Device Low Battery

* When a device detects low battery voltage.
* When the device’s low battery condition is restored.

### 389 – Calibration Failure

* Smoke Detector’s (SD) calibration fails.

### 393 – Maintenance Alert (SD-32)

* When the Smoke Detector SD-32 accumulates much dust and needs cleaning.

### 394 – Force Maintenance (SD-32)

* When the Smoke Detector SD-32 has accumulated too much dust, rendering it non-functional and requiring immediate cleaning.

### 400 – Arm / Disarm (by Remote Controller)

* When the system is armed or disarmed with the Remote Controller.

### 401 – Remote Arm / Disarm

* When the system is armed or disarmed by SMS messages or web access.

### 407 – Disarm / Away Arm / Home Arm by Remote Keypad

### 408 – Set / Unset Arm / Disarm

* When the DC set at Set/Unset is triggered or restored.

### 416 – Panel Firmware Update

* When the Panel’s firmware update is successfully executed on the Server.

### 429 – Program Mode Entry

* When the programming session starts for the panel on the server.

### 430 – Program Mode Exit

* When the programming session is terminated for the panel on the server.

### 456 – Partial Arm

* When the system is partially armed from Disarmed to Home Armed.

### 465 – Alarm Reset by SD/WS/CO/AQS

* After the device is triggered, the device button is pressed to cancel the alarm.

### 570 – Zone Bypass

* When fault exists in the system and the system is armed by ignoring the fault (arm fault type set to Direct Arm).

### 601 – Test Report

* When the Control Panel sends a Test Report.

### 602 – Periodic Test Report

* When the Control Panel makes period check-in reports.

### 616 – Call Request

* When the service call is activated by VST-809 / KPT-41.

### 693 – Cross Zone First Trip

* Device set to Cross Zone is triggered to start the Cross Zone Timer.

### 694 – Cross Zone Timeout

* When Cross Zone Timer expires after the alarm has been verified.

### 695 – Fire Verification Timeout

* When Fire Verification Timer expires.

### 705 – Medication Taken

* After the dosage has been dispensed and taken, a medication taken signal will be sent to the Control Panel.

### 706 – Medication Not Taken

* If the dose is still not taken 30 minutes after the schedule-dosage event, a dose-not –taken signal will be sent to the Control Panel.

### 707 – Dispenser's Tray Stuck

* When Pill Dispenser ‘s tray is stuck.
* When Dispenser's Tray Stuck status is cleared.

### 708 – Dispenser Upside Down

* When Pill Dispenser is upside down.
* When Dispenser’s upside down status is cleared.

### 750 – Trigger / Restore Notification

* When Door contact opens/closes or connected wired senor triggers/restores.

### 781 – Video Motion (for DAHUA IPCAM)

* When recording triggers upon any detected motion.

### 782 – Cross Line Detection (for DAHUA IPCAM)

* When line crossing is detected.

### 783 – Cross Region Detection (for DAHUA IPCAM)

* When region crossing is detected.

### 787 – IR Motion Detection (for IPCAM/VDP)

* When motion detected by the IR sensor of IP Camera or VDP.

### 788 – Motion Detection 2 (for IPCAM/VDP)

* When motion detection region 2 detects a movement.

### 789 – Motion Detection 1 (for IPCAM/VDP)

* When motion detection region 1 detects a movement.

### 790 – Doorbell Ring (for VDP)

* When VDP’s doorbell button is pressed.

### 791 – Missed Call (for VDP)

the doorbell ring is missed

### 792 – Answering Call (for VDP)

* When the doorbell ring is answered.

### 795 – Smart Human Motion Detection (for DAHUA IPCAM)

* When human motion is detected.

### 830 – Finished Liquid (for SFT-3)

* When Fog Security System detects finished liquid.

### 831 – Low Battery or Absent Battery (for SFT-3)

* When Fog Security System detects low battery voltage or battery absence.

### 832 – Defective Fog Canon Probe (for SFT-3)

* When Fog Security System detects defective fog canon probe.

### 833 – Lack of Electricity (for SFT-3)

* When Fog Security System detects a lack of electricity (AC Failure).

### 834 – Over-temperature of the Burner (for SFT-3)

* When Fog Security System detects the temperature of the burner being too high.

### 835 – Failure of Hydraulic Circuit (for SFT-3)

* When Fog Security System detects failure of the hydraulic circuit.

### 836 – Burner Failure (for SFT-3)

* When Fog Security System detects the burner failure.
