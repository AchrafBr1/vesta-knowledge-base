# Connect your Camera

**Ethernet**

Choose this option if your camera is connected using a LAN (Ethernet) cable.

&#x20;

**Wi-Fi**

Choose this option to connect your camera over Wi-Fi. The camera will create a temporary access point, allowing you to scan for available Wi-Fi networks and select yours.

&#x20;

**Camera already connected**

If your camera's LED is solid green, select this option. It indicates that the camera is already connected to the network.
